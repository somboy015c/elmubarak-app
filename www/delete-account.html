Delete your Elmubarak Digital Hub account

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information linked to it at any time.

In the Android app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap More at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and tap Request deletion.

Without the app

Message us on WhatsApp with the email address and phone number on your account.

What happens next

  • We contact you within 7 days to confirm the request and to pay out any money left in your wallet.
  • We then delete your name, email address, phone number, password, PINs, saved bank details and app sign-ins.
  • Records of past transactions may be kept for up to 6 years where the law or fraud prevention requires it, and are then deleted.

See our privacy policy for more details.
