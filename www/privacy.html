Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information Elmubarak Digital Hub ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and Android app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Transaction details: the services you buy (airtime, data, electricity, cable TV, exam PINs), the phone, meter or smartcard numbers you enter, amounts, and your wallet history.
  • Payment details: card, bank transfer and USSD payments are handled by Flutterwave. We never see or store your full card number or card PIN.
  • Withdrawal details: the bank name, account number and account name you give us for withdrawals.
  • Security information: your transaction PIN (stored as a secure hash), sign-in times, IP address and device type, used to protect your account and send sign-in alerts.
  • App PIN and fingerprint: if you turn on quick login, your 4-digit app PIN is stored only as a secure hash. Fingerprint checks are done by your phone; we never receive your fingerprint.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and wallet, and to deliver the services you buy.
  • To take and confirm payments and to pay out withdrawals.
  • To send you codes, receipts, security alerts and service notices.
  • To prevent fraud, keep your account secure and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run Elmubarak Digital Hub, and only what they need:

  • Flutterwave, to process payments and bank payouts.
  • Our airtime, data and bill-payment supplier, to deliver what you buy (for example the phone or meter number).
  • Our email provider, to send you codes, receipts and alerts.
  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the information securely.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Transaction records may be kept longer where needed for accounting, fraud prevention or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • You can update your details and change your password or PIN in the app or on the website.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time. See Delete your account.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and PINs, sign-in alerts and automatic sign-out to protect your account. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password and PINs private.

Children

Elmubarak Digital Hub is not intended for children under 13 years old.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
